ITSharkz: opening the French market for a Polish developer team

Dedicated developer teams for scale-ups and companies. Based in Poland.

ITSharkz
Business

Context

ITSharkz provides dedicated developer teams to scale-ups and companies, from Poland. To enter the French market, it needed to reach CTOs while they are hiring, with a system that runs every day.

Why signals

A funding round, a wave of tech hires, a developer role open for weeks: all signs that a team needs reinforcement fast.

The set-up

Targeting
French scale-ups and companies that need to grow their development teams
System
Prospecting run with Claude: a brief every morning, daily research, scheduled follow-ups
Pace
At least 15 new prospects a day, on top of follow-ups
Tools
A dedicated ITSharkz account, HubSpot and Apollo run end to end
Result
Meetings booked with French companies, reviewed every week with management

Ongoing since October 2026, as Head of Partnerships France, working hybrid from France.
