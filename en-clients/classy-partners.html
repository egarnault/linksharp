Classy Partners: finding restaurants at the moment they change

Dishes created by Meilleurs Ouvriers de France chefs, plus training and support for restaurant owners, delivered across France.

Client
Classy Partners
Business
Chef-made products for restaurants
Website
classy-partners.fr
Since
First LinkSharp client, since November 2026.

Context

Classy Partners delivers dishes created by Meilleurs Ouvriers de France chefs across France, and trains kitchen teams. Its best clients are restaurants at a specific moment: a chef leaving, a takeover, an opening. The rest of the time, a restaurant owner has neither the time nor the wish to listen to a new supplier.

Why signals

Kitchen job posts and business takeovers are public and dated. They show who is changing, and when. That's when keeping the menu consistent becomes a real issue.

The set-up

Targeting
Restaurants in transition, openings and takeovers, caterers, growing teams, across France
Signals
Head chef job posts, new management, a new location opening
Deliverables
Weekly batches of prospects with the venue's phone number and emails ready to approve
Training
A session so the team can research, verify and personalise on its own
New lead
Buyers of struggling restaurants, reached through referrers such as estate agents

Signals we watch

  • A venue opening or changing hands
  • A new chef or new management
  • Kitchen hiring
Real example, name removed

Restaurant, western France: hiring a lunchtime head chef “as part of a change of management”.

Subject: Your new lunchtime chef

Hello [First name],

I saw you're looking for a lunchtime head chef following the change of management. Keeping the same quality during the transition must be a real concern.

At Classy Partners, we deliver dishes created by MOF chefs across France, and we train kitchen teams.

Is this something you're working on right now?
