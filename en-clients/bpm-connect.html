BPM Connect: bringing venues into a booking app in Los Angeles

The booking app connecting artists, venues and fans. Launched in November 2026 in Los Angeles, on iOS and Android.

Context

BPM Connect is a booking app connecting artists, venues and fans, launched in November 2026 in Los Angeles. Like any marketplace, it has to attract both sides. Artists come easily. Venues, much less so.

Why signals

Venues that already book music, that are opening or that are changing their line-up are the ones with a reason to try a new way of booking.

The set-up

Instagram marketing
An editorial calendar on Los Angeles time, Reels every other day, carousel series, brand art direction.
Venue outreach
Restaurants, bars and clubs in Los Angeles: venue lists with contacts, an email campaign, a prospecting kit for the team. Goal: bring venues into the app.

Where things stand

Ongoing since October 2026, remotely from France.
