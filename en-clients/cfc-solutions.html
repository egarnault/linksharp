CFC Solutions: spotting companies whose receivables are overflowing

Friendly follow-up of every customer invoice, from the due date. No debt collection, no factoring. Lille.

Context

From Lille, CFC Solutions provides friendly follow-up of every customer invoice from the due date. No debt collection, no factoring: a service that makes sure invoices are received, approved and paid on time. The target is wide, but the right moment is precise.

Why signals

A company creating a credit manager role, replacing a retiring receivables clerk or hiring a treasurer is reorganising its receivables. That's when CFC can take over, during the transition or for good.

The set-up

Targeting
SMEs around Lille, B2C companies that also invoice businesses, public-sector suppliers
Signals
Longer payment times, late suppliers, a credit control job post spotted the day it goes live
Sending
From a dedicated CFC address, warmed up and tested before the first email
Pace
15 prospects a week, approved by CFC before each send
Meetings
Qualified with CFC's own grid, with a brief sent after each meeting

Signals we watch

  • A new credit manager role
  • A retirement in credit control
  • A treasurer being hired
