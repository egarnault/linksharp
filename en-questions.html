The questions nobody asks.

Ask them anyway. Here are my answers, straight.

“Where does your information come from?”

Public sources: business press, funding announcements, job postings, company websites, official registers. Every line in the file shows its source. You can check everything.

“And the email addresses?”

They come from established B2B data tools, and I verify them before delivering. A doubtful address is not used.

“Is this AI?”

Partly, yes. AI helps me search and draft the emails. I check the signal and the source of every company myself before it reaches you.

“Won't this hurt my mailbox?”

No. Few emails a day, spaced several minutes apart, sent from your own address. Never mass mailing.

“Is it legal?”

Yes. In France, the CNIL allows B2B email prospecting when the message relates to the person's job and lets them opt out. Every email does.

“Who owns my prospects?”

You do. The file is yours. I don't resell it and I don't reuse it for another client.

“How many meetings will I get?”

I don't promise a number. Nobody honest can. What I guarantee: 60 justified companies a month, and I replace any that don't fit.

“Why not do it myself?”

You can. But finding a real reason to write to each company takes hours. That time is what you are buying.

“Are you on your own?”

Yes. That's why I take few clients at a time. You always talk to the same person, the one doing the work.

“What if I don't like it?”

You stop at the end of the month. No commitment, no fees, no argument.

“How do I know you're good?”

Don't take my word for it. I show you three companies in your market, with the email ready, for free. Judge the work.

Another question?
