Client · Food service

First LinkSharp client, since November 2026.

classy-partners.fr

Classy Partners

Dishes created by Meilleurs Ouvriers de France chefs, plus training and support for restaurant owners, delivered across France.

Targeting
Restaurants in transition, openings and takeovers, caterers, growing teams, across France
Signals
Head chef job posts, new management, a new location opening
Deliverables
Weekly batches of prospects with the venue's phone number and emails ready to approve
Training
A session so the team can research, verify and personalise on its own
New lead
Buyers of struggling restaurants, reached through referrers such as estate agents

Client · Software

LinkSharp client since November 2026.

inekto

Takeover, rebuild and maintenance of business software, at a fixed price. Paris and Aix-en-Provence.

Targeting
CIOs and leaders of mid-sized companies whose business software costs too much or has become risky
Signals
ERP upgrades, AS400 or COBOL developers sought for months, licence costs climbing
Deliverables

Qualification
Written qualification rules, shared with the sales team
Integration
Handover to inekto's SDR, who takes over each batch

CFC Solutions

Friendly follow-up of every customer invoice, from the due date. No debt collection, no factoring. Lille.

Targeting
SMEs around Lille, B2C companies that also invoice businesses, public-sector suppliers
Signals
Longer payment times, late suppliers, a credit control job post spotted the day it goes live
Sending
From a dedicated CFC address, warmed up and tested before the first email
Pace
15 prospects a week, approved by CFC before each send
Meetings
Qualified with CFC's own grid, with a brief sent after each meeting
