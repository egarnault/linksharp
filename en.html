The method

Five checks before every prospect.

A company that fails one check is dropped, and the reason is logged. The daily report shows which criterion limits your volume.

✓
Target · area, sector and exclusions respected
✓
Signal · a public, dated fact, with its source
✓
Decision-maker · still in the role, confirmed by a recent source
✓
Email · verified or officially published, never guessed
✓
Duplicate · one person per company, never contacted twice
Your data

No hosted software. Your data stays with you.

Your prospects, emails and calendar stay in your own Google account. LinkSharp is a window onto your tracking sheet, not a database. It requires Gmail and Google Calendar: Outlook and Microsoft 365 are not supported yet during the beta.

The AI part runs in the assistant you already pay for (Claude, ChatGPT or another), with your own connections, which you can revoke at any time.

Every email ends with a one-line way to say stop, and any opt-out is applied immediately.

Export

Prospects abroad: language, local time and public holidays are handled.

What LinkSharp does not do: send without your approval, buy contact lists, use a fact without a source, or promise a number of meetings. Like any AI, it can make mistakes, which is why you review every batch. Results depend on your target and your offer; LinkSharp helps you test them properly.
