    Background

    A simple method, done by hand.

    Every week, about 15 companies per client, with the decision-maker, the sourced signal and the email ready.

    The method in detail

    1. I spot.

      A funding round, a new hire, an opening, a new leader. That's often when a company buys.

    2. I write.

      A short email for each one, built on a verified, dated fact. Never copy and paste.

    3. You send.

      You read, you approve, the email goes from your address. Or I handle sending and follow-ups.
